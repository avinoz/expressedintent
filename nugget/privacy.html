Nugget Log · Effective November 12, 2026

Privacy Policy

Nugget Log helps a family keep one shared log of a baby's day. We collect only what the app needs to do that, we never sell it, and we never use it for advertising.

Who we are

Nugget Log is made by Side Table LLC, which does business as Expressed Intent. When this policy says "we" or "us," it means Side Table LLC. You can reach us at alvin@expressedintent.com.

What we collect

  • Account details. When you sign in with Apple, we receive a user identifier and, if you choose to share them, your name and email address. Apple may give us a private relay email instead of your real one.
  • Your display name and icon, so other caregivers in your family can see who logged what.
  • Family details: the family name, its members and their roles (parent, family or nanny), and invitations you create.
  • Your baby's profile: name, birthday, gender (optional) and avatar.
  • Logs you enter: feeds, sleep, diapers, solids, medicine, pumping, growth measurements and any notes you add, with the time and the caregiver who logged them.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, browsing activity or device advertising identifier. Nugget Log contains no advertising and no third-party analytics.

How we use it

We use this information only to run the app: to sign you in, keep your family's log in sync across caregivers' devices, show feed and sleep suggestions based on your baby's age and history, and send the reminders you turn on. We do not use it to track you across other apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent it to anyone.

Where it is stored

Your family's data is stored on your device and synced through two services that act on our behalf:

  • Supabase, which hosts our database and sign-in service. Access is limited so that only members of a family can read that family's data.
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit), which Nugget Log uses to share your family's log with the caregivers you invite.

Data is encrypted in transit. Reminders are local notifications created on your iPhone; their content is not sent to us.

Who can see it

Only the caregivers you invite to your family can see your family's data. Anyone you invite sees the full shared log. You can remove a caregiver at any time from Settings → Family. We do not share your data with anyone else, except where the law requires it.

Children's privacy

Nugget Log is meant for parents and other adult caregivers, not for children to use. Information about a baby is entered by their parent or caregiver and is used only to provide the app to that family.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. In Settings you can:

  • Delete your account, which removes your login and your data on that device. Other caregivers keep the family.
  • Delete your family's account, which erases the household, every baby and every log for all caregivers.

Deleted data is removed from our systems promptly, apart from routine backups, which expire on their normal schedule. You can also email us to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.

Contact
