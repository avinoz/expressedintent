Delete your account

You can delete your Nugget Log account and the data tied to it at any time, in the app on Android or iPhone, or by emailing us. This page explains each way and exactly what is removed.

Delete it in the app

This is the fastest way, and it takes effect immediately.

  1. Open Nugget Log and go to Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm. Your account is deleted and you are signed out.

Delete your whole family

If you created the family, you can instead erase the entire household for every caregiver.

  1. Open Nugget Log and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete family account, which only the family's owner can see.
  3. Type the family name to confirm. The family, every baby and every log are erased for all caregivers. Other people keep their own Nugget Log login.

Can't open the app? Email us

Send a message to alvin@expressedintent.com with the subject Delete my Nugget Log account, from the email address you use to sign in. If you signed in with Google or Apple, use that account's email address. If you only have a private relay address, tell us and we will verify you another way.

We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days, usually much sooner. Tell us whether you want only your account removed or your whole family erased.

What is deleted

  • Deleting your account removes your login, your display name, your sign-in identifiers, and your place in the family. If you own the family and other caregivers are in it, ownership moves to another caregiver and the family continues. If you are the only member, the family, its babies and all its logs are deleted too.
  • Deleting the family removes the family, its members' links to it, every baby profile, and every feed, sleep, diaper, solids, medicine, pumping and growth entry, with their notes and invitations.
  • Data stored on your device is wiped when you sign out of a deleted account.

What we keep

Nothing about you or your family is kept once deletion is complete, apart from routine encrypted backups, which expire on their normal schedule. If the law requires us to keep a record, we keep only that record, and only as long as required. Deleted data cannot be recovered.
